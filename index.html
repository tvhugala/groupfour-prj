    Welcome to the department of computer sciences

    What do I get from learning computer sciences?

    The Computer Sciences field will help you know and understand how things work. It's not limited to only coding as we also have computer architecture, computing concepts, algorithms, and more that we won't mention as this field is so broad. You can pick any career field of choice as you progress through, but as the university of Limpopo, we mostly focus on software development (software engineering).
