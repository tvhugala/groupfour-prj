    • About the Computer Sciences Department

    Our department started out as a small computer lab on campus and has grown over the years into a vibrant hub for tech innovation and learning. We've watched the tech world change drastically and have continually updated our approach to help train the next generation of software developers and digital creators.

    History of the department

    Vision

    We want to be a leading place for computer science learning and research, creating tech solutions that actually make a difference in everyday life.

    To give students practical, high-quality training in software development and computer science.
    To encourage curiosity, out-of-the-box thinking, and good ethical habits when building technology.
    To team up with local tech companies and industries so our students get real-world exposure.
